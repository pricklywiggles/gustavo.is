# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who lands on gustavo.is — no single visitor type is gatekept, and the site reads broadly rather than narrowly targeting one persona. The sub-group whose reaction actually matters is whoever is sizing Gustavo up for a role or engagement: a hiring manager, a founder, a recruiter, a potential collaborator. They typically arrive from a resume, a referral, or a search, already curious about the background, and are deciding whether reaching out is worth their time.

## Product Purpose

A personal site — about, portfolio, and blog — that presents Gustavo Gallegos's work and background and converts a visit into direct contact. Success is a visitor deciding to start a conversation through the contact form; the writing and work sections exist to build the case that gets them there.

## Positioning

26 years (since 2000) operating across product management, QA, engineering program management, and hands-on software development, at companies including Microsoft (Office, VSTO), Slide.com (SuperPoke Pets, Top Friends), Jawbone, Ponder, and Tartle. As AI collapses the value of narrow specialization, that full-spectrum range across the entire product lifecycle is the compounding advantage, not a lack of focus.

**This is a strategic claim, not a line of copy.** The site should never state "I'm a new kind of generalist" outright — it demonstrates the range through the work itself (case studies that show PM thinking, QA rigor, program management, and engineering side by side) and lets a visitor arrive at the conclusion on their own.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: the contact form. No secondary/fallback CTA — one clear ask, not diluted by a lower-commitment alternative.
- Belief ladder a visitor should climb, in order: (1) this is senior-level range, not junior scattershot — backed by recognizable companies; (2) that range means sitting on every side of the table — product, quality, program, and code; (3) AI makes that range more valuable now, not less; (4) reaching out costs nothing and the upside is high.
- Proof on hand: company/product logos collected in `.impeccable/assets/proof/` — Microsoft, Office, VSTO, Slide.com, SuperPoke Pets, Top Friends, Jawbone, Ponder, Tartle. No testimonials or case studies yet; the work section carries that weight until some exist.

## Brand Personality

Playful and distinctive, warm and approachable, precise and meticulous. The precision is already evidenced by this codebase's own rigor (documented stack decisions, version pins, supply-chain vetting); the playfulness and warmth come from personal touches like the custom Lego-avatar hero concept rather than corporate polish. Confident, not hyped — the range and the work carry the argument, the tone doesn't oversell it.

## Anti-references

The cookie-cutter developer-portfolio template look — the GitHub-README-as-website pattern shared by most dev portfolios (generic hero, identical project cards, no personal voice).

## Design Principles

- **Show range, don't state it.** Every section demonstrates PM, QA, program management, and engineering thinking through what it contains and how it's built, never through a label or a claim of breadth.
- **Precision with warmth.** Meticulous execution paired with personality — never cold, never corporate, never sloppy.
- **One clear ask.** Every page points at the contact form; nothing competes with it for the visitor's next action.
- **Proof over adjectives.** Recognizable companies and real work carry the positioning; the copy doesn't have to.
- **Motion tells the story.** The site is motion-heavy by intent (GSAP for scroll-driven storytelling, Motion for component animation): the landing page narrates through scroll, and microinteractions appear wherever tasteful — motion is part of the craft argument, never decoration.
- **Practice what you preach.** The site's own craft — performance, accessibility, motion — is itself evidence for the positioning claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: contrast, full keyboard navigation, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation — relevant immediately given the GSAP-driven parallax hero concept already in progress.
